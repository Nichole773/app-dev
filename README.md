## My favorite series ##

**The Tall Girl**
> Tall Girl is a 2019 American teen romantic comedy film directed by Nzingha Stewart, from a screenplay by Sam Wolfson. The film stars Ava Michelle, Griffin Gluck, Sabrina Carpenter, Luke Eisner, Paris Berelc, Clara Wilsey, Anjelika Washington, Rico Paris, Angela Kinsey, and Steve Zahn. The film was released by Netflix on September 13, 2019, and received mixed reviews. It later got a sequel released in February 2022.

**Plot**

*Since she was three years old, sixteen-year-old Jodi Kreyman has been tall for her age, which has caused her to feel insecure all of her life. Students make fun of her height on a regular basis. Harper, Jodi's older sister, has won several beauty pageants despite being short in stature. She is reluctant when her lifelong friend Jack Dunkleman repeatedly asks her out, in part because he is significantly shorter than her. When Stig Mohlin, a Swedish exchange student, enrolls in Jodi's class, she and the majority of the other girls are instantly drawn to him. But Kimmy Stitcher, her bully, takes him on a tour. When Dunkleman finds out that Stig will be staying at his home as the host family, he becomes upset. Jodi requests Harper's assistance in getting Stig.
